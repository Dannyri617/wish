1. **🎁 The Mystery Gift Box Unboxing**:

2. **🎂 Interactive Birthday Cake & Candles**:
   - Multi-tier pastel frosting cake with glowing, animated candles.

7. **🎵 Built-in Birthday Melody**:
   - Web Audio synthesizer that plays a soothing, music-box style *Happy Birthday* tune without needing external MP3 files. Includes mute/unmute toggle.

8. **✏️ In-Browser Customizer**:
   - Click the pencil icon (`✏️`) in the top-right corner to easily upload your own photos directly from your computer, change text, or edit the letter right on your screen!
